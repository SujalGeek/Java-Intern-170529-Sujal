## Understanding Crew AI and Multi-Agent Systems

- **Crew AI** is a Python framework designed to create **multi-agent systems (MAS)** or workflows where multiple AI agents interact and collaborate.

- **Multi-Agent Systems:**  
  Systems composed of multiple interacting intelligent agents working towards common or individual goals.

---

## Journey from Large Language Models (LLMs) to AI Agents

- **LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT):**  
  - Large Language Models trained on massive datasets (almost entire internet data) to understand and generate natural language.  
  - Operate using **next word prediction**: generating text word-by-word based on user prompts.  
  - Excellent for chatbots due to their conversational abilities.

- **Development Cycle:**  
  - LLMs evolved through reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), improving based on user prompts and feedback (thumbs up/down).  
  - This evolution paved the way for transforming LLMs into **AI agents** capable of autonomous tasks beyond simple conversation.
